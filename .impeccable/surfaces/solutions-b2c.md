# Surface brief: /solutions/b2c and homepage Consumers view

Mode: Persuade.
Visitor: a business that runs on an ERP and sells to consumers through a web shop (WooCommerce, Shopify or similar, with plugins and a connector). Decision: book the free consultation.
Story (agreed 8 Oct 2026): "Stop using your current consumer shop and switch to us." Six reasons in order (orders reach ERP, checkout price is ERP price, live stock, item master to shop, updates stop breaking it, month end matched), then why switch not patch, then switching is safe, FAQ, consultation form. Nothing else.
World: credible clean software, house navy and green, Space Grotesk / Poppins / Red Hat Mono. Not loud industrial, not generic SaaS card grids. Not the B2B problem-page template.
Device: each reason is a concrete consumer moment drawn as an HTML/CSS mock (product page, basket, order confirmation, stock, variants, update log, month-end match). Mocks are labelled illustrative. Mono type only for ERP data.
Rules: no em dashes, no invented stats, quotes, customers or prices, no ERP named, no B2B portal or part codes.
Files: src/components/b2c/*, public/css/b2c.css, src/data/b2c.ts.
