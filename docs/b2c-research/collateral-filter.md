# What from the proposal and flyer earns a place on the B2C page

Sources: Haus der Guten Weine proposal (AB-2026-00416-SUB, 1 Oct 2026) and the event flyer (B2Bware Kunden Flyer EN), checked against the B2C research in this folder (8 Oct 2026).

Rule: a point goes on the page only if the research shows owners complain about it. Being in our collateral is not evidence.

## The mismatch to settle

The research describes small store owners (2 to 50 staff) drowning in plugins. The proposal is for a business with an ERP (Navision, moving to Business Central) selling to consumers and hospitality trade, at about EUR 22,000 set-up and EUR 548 a month. The page should speak to the second group: businesses with an ERP whose consumer shop runs on WooCommerce. The research pains still apply to them; the price rules out the small owner.
