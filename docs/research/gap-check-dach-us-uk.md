# Gap check: DACH, US, UK (service providers, not just software)

Date: 23 Sep 2026. Adversarial check of the claim: "nobody offers all three routes (ERP-connected B2B portal, emailed/PDF orders into the ERP, fixing ERP-to-webshop connectors) as one done-for-you service at a published fixed price."

Method note: web search budget for the session ran out part way through, so some named targets (Sana DACH partners, Esker, Kofax/Tungsten, Parashift, Lobster, SEEBURGER, Alumio, e-invoicing 2027 providers, Space48, Vaimo, Swanky, Blue Bear, Celigo managed services page, Jitterbit, Boomi partners, SuiteCommerce agencies, Pepperi, Handshake) were NOT verified in this pass. Absence from the table below means "not checked", not "does not exist". Everything in the table has a URL.

Routes key: P = B2B portal/webshop connected to ERP. E = emailed/PDF orders into ERP. F = fix/run ERP-to-webshop connectors (integration build and managed support).

## Verdict per region

### DACH: gap is weakest here
- Service providers doing portal plus ERP integration with PUBLISHED fixed prices and monthly fees exist: B2B Commerce Agentur (39,000 EUR shop with ERP, 249 to 990 EUR/month) and breadcrumb (19,000 to 30,000 EUR B2B Shopify on Business Central, from 290 EUR/month, fixed price after workshop). So "nobody publishes fixed build plus monthly" is FALSE for route P plus F in DACH.
- At least two agencies cover all three routes as services: P&M Agentur (B2B e-commerce, portals, ERP interfaces, own OrderAI with published 8,000 EUR integration fee, "fixed-price guarantee") and 2Hats Logic (Shopware shops, ERP integration, custom AI email-order automation). Neither packages the three as ONE offer with one price, and P&M targets large clients (Siemens Energy, Otto, Edeka).
- Route E is crowded with software: Workist, Turian, it.conex re:order, BI2run SmartOrders, Lleverage (which is itself service-heavy, forward-deployed).

### US: gap holds only narrowly, and software is closing it
- DCKAP already sells portal (Commerce), AI order entry from email, and a managed integration product (Integrator, "fully managed setup and support", flat plans). That is all three routes from one vendor, though sold as products for distributors on specific ERPs (P21, Acumatica etc.), not as a bespoke done-for-you service, and prices are not all published.
- WizCommerce, OrderEase and StackCube each combine portal plus email/PDF order capture plus ERP sync. StackCube publishes 79 to 479 USD/month. OrderEase sells fixed-price implementation packages.
- Done-for-you plus flat monthly is an established US model: CIMcloud (15,000 USD plus 500 USD/month), Website Pipeline (from 500 USD/month), Corsica (flat-rate managed EDI/ERP). So "fixed build plus monthly" is not new in the US.

### UK: gap holds best, but is thin
- UK players are mostly software for route P (SparkLayer, B2B Wave, GOb2b, Aphix, commercebuild, Modulus365, Portal People). Williams Commerce is the closest service provider for P plus F (own middleware, managed services) but publishes no prices and shows no email-order route.
- No UK service provider found in this pass that covers route E as a service combined with P and F. Several UK-known agencies (Space48, Vaimo, Swanky, Blue Bear) were not verified.

## Which parts of the claim survive

| Claim element | Status | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| "Nobody offers all three routes" | FALSE as worded. DCKAP (US), P&M Agentur (DE), 2Hats Logic (DE) cover all three in some form; WizCommerce and OrderEase cover two plus ERP sync | Rows above |
| "as one done-for-you service" | PARTLY TRUE. The combos found are either software (DCKAP, WizCommerce, OrderEase) or bespoke agency projects (P&M, 2Hats). None found packaging the three as one managed product | Rows above |
| "at a published fixed price" | PARTLY TRUE. Published fixed build plus monthly exists for portal-only or portal plus ERP (B2B Commerce Agentur, breadcrumb, CIMcloud, Website Pipeline). No one found publishing a fixed price for the three-route bundle | Rows above |
| "SME focus, 15 to 200 staff" | WEAK differentiator. OrderDrafter targets 5 to 50 staff, StackCube and B2B Wave are SMB-priced, breadcrumb and B2B Commerce Agentur target Mittelstand | Rows above |
| "connects to almost any ERP quickly" | WEAK differentiator. Turian, it.conex and Conexiom (40+ ERPs) make the same "almost any ERP" claim | Rows above |

Real differentiator, if any: the packaging, i.e. one managed service, one fixed build price, one flat monthly fee, covering all three order routes, sold to SMEs across mixed ERPs. That specific bundle was not found. But each component is available, and a buyer can assemble it from one DACH agency plus a SaaS order-capture tool, or buy DCKAP in the US. The defensible angle is "one accountable team for every way orders reach your ERP", not "nobody does this".

Most crowded: route E (email/PDF order automation), with at least 11 vendors found here across all three regions, several with self-serve SMB pricing (StackCube 79 USD/month, OrderDrafter 399 USD/month). Then route P in the UK (SaaS portals from 135 GBP/month).

## Pricing benchmarks (to test the fixed-price model)

Build / setup:
- DACH portal with ERP: 14,900 to 49,000 EUR (B2B Commerce Agentur); 19,000 to 30,000 EUR (breadcrumb, B2B Shopify on BC); 80,000 to 150,000 EUR (nuonic, complex B2B).
- DACH ERP standard connector: from 4,900 EUR (B2B Commerce Agentur).
- DACH AI email-order integration: 8,000 EUR one-off (P&M OrderAI).
- US portal with ERP: from 15,000 USD (CIMcloud); 20,000 to 100,000 USD (Website Pipeline enterprise).
- US managed EDI/ERP: 2,000 to 50,000 USD (Corsica).
- Spain SME portal: 3,000 to 12,000 EUR (ASD Solutions), a low-end signal.

Monthly:
- Agency maintenance DACH: 249 to 990 EUR (B2B Commerce Agentur), from 290 EUR (breadcrumb), from 1,000 EUR (nuonic).
- US managed portal/integration: from 500 USD (CIMcloud, Website Pipeline), from 1,250 USD (DCKAP Integrator), 300 to 10,000 USD (Corsica).
- SaaS portals: 49 to 499 USD (SparkLayer), 135 GBP (B2B Wave), 684 to 5,464 USD (commercebuild).
- SaaS email-order capture: 79 to 479 USD (StackCube), 399 to 799 USD (OrderDrafter), from 18,000 USD/yr (Rossum), 10k to 100k+ USD/yr (Conexiom, third-party estimate).
- iPaaS: Celigo median 14,706 USD/yr (Vendr).
- Day rates: 940 to 950 EUR (DACH agencies); Upwork senior Shopify/ERP devs 80 to 150 USD/h (https://www.upwork.com/hire/shopify-developers/cost/ and third-party summaries).

Implication: a fixed build in the 10,000 to 40,000 EUR/GBP range plus 300 to 1,500 per month is squarely in line with what DACH and US buyers already see published. The price model is validated as normal, not novel. Publishing it is a mild differentiator in the UK, where most portal vendors hide price or sell SaaS only.
