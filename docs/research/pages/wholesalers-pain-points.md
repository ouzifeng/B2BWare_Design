## Claims to back up

Every capability claim on the page. Confirm each with the team before launch.

1. Phone orders captured and put into the ERP.
2. Emails, PDFs and spreadsheets read, customer codes matched to ours.
3. WhatsApp orders read and put into the ERP (add-on at a flat monthly fee).
4. Online portal with each customer's own prices and one-click reorder.
5. Reps order with live prices (sales app, add-on in b2b.ts).
6. Prices, stock and accounts checked against the ERP before the order posts.
7. Finished sales orders posted to the ERP; team checks exceptions, not every line.
8. Every customer sees and is charged their own price, live from the ERP.
9. Live sync of prices, stock and orders, on any ERP.
10. We take over a broken link or build it new; fixes after ERP updates are in the monthly fee, no day rates.
11. We set up accounts, invite customers with the client, and check who is ordering after 30 days.
12. Cut-off times handled: late orders go on the next run.
13. Delivery days per customer handled: the order lands on the right one.
14. Standing and repeat orders go in at current prices.
15. Promotions and time-limited prices come from the ERP and switch off on their end date.
16. Works on Sage 50, Sage 200, Business Central, NetSuite or any other ERP; a missing link is built in set-up.
17. Every correction is kept, so matching improves.
18. Live in weeks, not months.
19. One team owns it all.
20. Pricing: GBP 5,000 set-up, GBP 300 a month, first 1,000 orders included, about 15p after; 1,300 orders is about GBP 345 a month; no per-user fees; custom work quoted fixed first.
21. "Already running for manufacturers and distributors in the UK and Austria" (Kienesberger, Harrows, Doppler).

Items 12 to 15 (and phone capture, item 1) were flagged "not confirmed" in the brief, then approved by David in the task. Confirm the detail, especially how phone orders are captured and how time-limited prices are read from each ERP.
