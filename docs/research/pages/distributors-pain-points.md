## Claims to back up

Every capability claim on the page, for the team to confirm before launch.

1. Emailed PDF and spreadsheet orders are read and posted to the ERP as finished sales orders, checked against prices, stock and credit, awaiting approval.
2. Orders are read overnight (timeline 8am slot). Confirm this timing, or reword.
3. Customer codes are mapped to ours, and every correction is kept so mistakes do not repeat.
4. Phone and WhatsApp orders are captured into the ERP (WhatsApp is also listed as a flat-fee add-on on the price list).
5. Each account sees its own prices, range and terms live from the ERP, across thousands of SKUs (20,000 is used only as an example SKU count in a timeline heading).
6. Live sync of prices, stock and orders with the ERP.
7. Live stock across branches and warehouses (brief says branch-level stock is unconfirmed).
8. Works with any ERP, built if not covered. SyncSpider run for 10 years (from existing b2b.ts FAQ).
9. ERP updates are fixed in the monthly fee.
10. Supplier product data is loaded for the customer (brief says this is not a core service: confirm).
11. Punchout ordering from customers' own buying systems, at their own prices, into the ERP (brief says not in the listed offer: confirm cXML/OCI delivery).
12. EDI is an add-on at a flat monthly fee.
13. We set up accounts and invite customers.
14. Live in weeks, not months. One team owns all three fixes.
15. Pricing: from £5,000 set up, £300 a month, first 1,000 orders included, then about 15p an order, no per-user fees. The worked example (3,000 orders is about £600 a month) is arithmetic from those figures.
16. Kienesberger: general distributor for several brands, Austria; each customer's own prices from the ERP in their portal, updated daily in about 15 minutes; old shop would have needed over 20 million price entries.
17. Illustrative mock (fictional names, "Illustrative example.") shows Leeds branch stock, their code mapped to ours, credit check.
