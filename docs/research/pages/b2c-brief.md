# /solutions/b2c page brief (8 Oct 2026)

Evidence: research/pages/b2c-pain-points.md. Read it first. Only its pains go on the page.

## The problem, in one line
You run on an ERP and you have a consumer web shop, and they don't work together. The shop was built on a general commerce core with plugins bolted on. It was never built around your ERP, so prices, stock, products and orders drift apart and someone fixes it by hand.

## Who it's for
Businesses that run on an ERP (wholesalers, manufacturers, merchants) selling to consumers online, often to trade as well. Any ERP. Never name one.

## Page story
1. Hero: the shop and the ERP don't work together. Candidate: "Your web shop wasn't built for your ERP" + highlight "so your team holds them together". Sub: orders retyped or missing, prices and VAT that don't match, stock that oversells. We build the shop on your ERP and run it.
2. Symptoms (pains 1 to 6, buyer words): order never arrived and the customer called; price or promotion differs; stock shows what you don't have; product details don't reach the shop; the link broke after an update; month end matching payments by hand.
3. Why it happens: the root cause. A general shop core plus plugins and a connector copying data on a timer. Three or four parts, three or four vendors, nobody built it around your ERP. Show it, don't lecture (a drawn stack vs one system).
4. Your options, honestly: ERP's own shop (dies when the ERP changes), shop + plugin or connector (timer sync, one price), agency build (expensive, tied to the agency), us. Short table, no brand names needed beyond WooCommerce/Shopify as shop examples.
5. How we fix it: shop built on your item master, prices, VAT and stock from the ERP, every order lands in the ERP and we watch it, updates in the fee, change ERP and we reconnect it. Trade customers on the same system if you sell to both.
6. Switching: what moves (products, customers, orders, reviews, redirects so rankings hold), what is rebuilt (design), customers reset passwords at go-live, live in about 2 months, exit before go-live paying only hours worked.
7. Proof: SyncSpider, 10 years, 400+ integrations. No B2C customer names unless confirmed.
8. FAQ: will it work with our ERP; do we lose Google rankings; can trade and consumers share it; what if the ERP changes; who fixes it when it breaks; what does it cost (fixed price agreed before we start, quoted in the consultation).
9. CTA: Book a free consultation (same form as the homepage, #order-check pattern).

## Out
GDPR, EU hosting, data centre, AI hosting, accessibility, social/ads hub, loyalty, app, POS, MCP, PageSpeed as a headline, named ERPs, a public B2C price.
