## Claims to back up

Each is approved by David for plain statement, but needs a named owner to confirm before launch.

1. Prices, stock and orders sync live with the ERP (hero mock, pain 3, FAQ).
2. Each dealer sees their own prices, range and terms, live from the ERP.
3. Connects to any ERP, including manufacturing ERPs like Epicor, SYSPRO, Infor and IFS (FAQ, fit section). The brief says per-ERP connectors are unconfirmed, so check each.
4. When the ERP updates, we fix the link, included in the monthly fee, no day rates.
5. Emailed, PDF and spreadsheet orders are read, matched to the manufacturer's codes, checked against the ERP and posted as finished sales orders, with team approval first.
6. Phone and WhatsApp orders are captured into the ERP (WhatsApp ordering is an add-on on the pricing list).
7. Big customers can order from inside their own buying system (punchout).
8. We set up accounts and invite dealers, and check who is ordering after 30 days.
9. Live in weeks, not months.
10. Sales app for field agents is an add-on, flat fee, not priced per agent.
11. Agents order for dealers with live ERP prices in the same portal.
12. Price: £5,000 to set up, £300 a month, first 1,000 orders included, about 15p after (from shared pricing data).
13. Doppler: sales agents order with live ERP pricing. Harrows Darts: trade customers order through a portal connected to the ERP. Kienesberger: each customer's own prices from the ERP, updated daily in about 15 minutes.
14. SyncSpider has run the integration platform for 10 years (FAQ, reused from shared FAQ text).
15. Pain quotes (pains 1 to 3) are taken from r/manufacturing threads in the brief. Pain 4 line is unquoted copy, not a customer quote.
16. Fit section: right for standard coded products on account; not for configured or one-off priced products.
