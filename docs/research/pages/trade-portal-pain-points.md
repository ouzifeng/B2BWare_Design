# Trade portal: verified pain points vs page copy

Source: research/pages/trade-portal-brief.md (7 Oct 2026). Copy: landing/src/data/solutions.ts, 'trade-portal'. For presenting to the CEO.

## Pain on the supplier's side

| # | Pain point | Best evidence | Strength |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Same reorders taken by phone and email, again and again | US distributor: "a unique list of around 30-50 items they order routinely"; SDR call, Company of Animals: 4 staff, ~100 orders/week, small accounts are the pain | Strong |
| 2 | Orders retyped into the ERP | "manually key it into sage50" | Strong |
| 3 | Webshop or portal exists but is not linked to the ERP, or shows the wrong price | Aztec Oils (agency portal, no Sage link); Kienesberger (Shopware would have needed 20M price entries); r/manufacturing "Customers see stale stock, wrong pricing" | Medium-strong |
| 4 | Customer-specific pricing breaks the cheap options | Shopify 3 catalogues without Plus; Plus $2,300/month "not affordable for us"; BC connector "struggles to sync customer-specific pricing" (vendor-written), rated 2.6/5; NetSuite "B2B gets messy fast" | Medium-strong |
| 5 | Overnight sync is not good enough | UK Magento + Sage 200 wholesaler: "That is not integration in my book" | One source, UK, exact ICP |
| 6 | Ticket-queue support and vendors blaming each other | GOb2b reviews: "Any customisation has to be completed by the support team via a ticketing system"; "all the developers point the finger at the other apps" | Medium |
| 7 | ERP update breaks the link | BC 26.0 broke a UK merchant's sync | One instance |
| 8 | Fear of a big IT project and an unclear price | SDR calls: price asked early, per-head pricing hated; agency post-mortem: calls "dropped off immediately once pricing or custom development timelines were introduced" | Medium |
| 9 | Reps tied up taking orders | 7 vendors list it, none lead; Doppler | Medium-weak |

## Pain on the customer's side (why portals go quiet)

| # | Pain point | Best evidence | Strength |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | Customer sees a price that is not their deal and rings anyway | Sana-commissioned survey: 29% held back by inaccurate pricing, 55% want agreed prices; Kibo "a unit of trust" | Medium |
| 11 | Portal built, then unused | "only 12%" case; "technically live and commercially unused". Drivers: right price, faster than phoning, reps on side, someone onboards customers | Medium (drivers), low (numbers). Never publish an adoption number |

## Weak, do not lead with
- Customers asking to order online: ranked 8th of 11.
- Losing customers to competitors with portals: last, 1 mention; 68-78% switching figures are stated intent from a vendor survey.
- Trade-counter sectors: 92% of builders' merchant trade sales over the counter. Poor fit.

Caveat: UK buyer voice is thin (one Sage 200 wholesaler, GOb2b reviews 2021-22). Most evidence is US forums and vendor surveys.

Weak pain used as a symptom: 'A customer asks: "Can I just order online?"'

Team question (7 Oct 2026): hero now says "Live stock levels". Confirm stock is live for every ERP we connect; if not, change to "Stock levels".

## Claims to back up (written in to answer researched pain, 7 Oct 2026)
- "Live stock levels" (hero) and "Prices and stock stay in step with your ERP through the day, not in an overnight file" (pain 5)
- "Off-the-shelf portals carry a few price lists. They break when every customer has its own price." (pain 4; Shopify 3-catalogue limit, Kienesberger)
- "When your ERP updates, we fix the link, and that is in the monthly fee" (pain 7)
- "We set up every account, invite your customers with you, and check who is ordering after 30 days" (pain 11; also Launch step and FAQ)
- "Live in weeks, not months" (pain 8; How it works intro, shared by all three pages)
