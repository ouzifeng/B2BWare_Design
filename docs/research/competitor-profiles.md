## Synthesis
1. **Closest overlap:** GOb2b and Codeless, both UK and Sage 200-first. Both sell software, so the customer or their partner does the work.
2. **Next:** Sana and k-eCommerce (Business Central portals) and Workist (orders into Sage 200 and Business Central). None of them combines a portal with order capture.
3. **Nobody offers all three routes into the ERP as one done-for-you service** for Sage 200 or Business Central SMEs: portal, captured orders and connector repair.
4. **The order-capture players** have enterprise or EU customers and no UK SME Sage story.
5. **Nobody publishes a fixed build price plus a flat monthly fee** for this buyer.
