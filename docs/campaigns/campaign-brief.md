# B2Bware campaign brief (UK, English). Read together with site-brief.md.

## Goal
One conversion everywhere: request a free order check (send a week of real orders, see how many would land in the ERP automatically). Runway is 2 to 3 months, so the plan must find a winning angle fast.
Budget: not given. Plan in percentages: about 60% emailed orders, 30% fix the link, 10% trade portal. Channels: Google Ads and Meta. LinkedIn only as an optional note. Germany is a separate later campaign, out of scope here.

## Audience
Operations, sales or finance directors and owners at UK manufacturers, distributors and wholesalers, 15 to 200 staff, stocked coded products, a known ERP (Sage 200, Sage 50, Business Central, NetSuite, IFS, SAP Business One), dozens of trade orders a week or more, a team keying them in.
Bad fits (exclude in targeting and negatives): bespoke or configured products, a handful of orders a week, firms mid ERP migration, consumer retail, jobseekers ("order entry clerk jobs"), students, free/DIY seekers.

## The three angles (each has its own ads and its own landing page)
| # | Angle | Landing page | Hook in buyers' words | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Emailed orders | `emailed-orders.html` | "Still keying PDF orders into Sage?" / "Every order in your ERP. Nobody retyping it." | Strongest, most common pain in UK calls and buyer threads |
| 2 | Fix the link you have | `fix-your-link.html` | "Shop and ERP out of step again?" / "We take over the link someone else built, and keep it working." | Clear trigger (it broke), best evidence people pay someone to fix it; searched at the moment of pain |
| 3 | Trade portal | `trade-portal.html` | "Trade customers see their own prices online, and the order lands in your ERP." | Real but crowded (Shopify B2B, native connectors); small, exact-intent only |

Top buyer worries: will it go into the ERP or just read the order; accuracy (want human approval first); price up front, dislike opaque pricing; we already have a reading tool.

## Claims allowed in ads
Price is published: from £5,000 set-up, then £300 a month with 1,000 orders included. Free order check. Every route, one team, we fix it when it breaks. Proof: Doppler (70% faster order entry), Kienesberger, Harrows Darts. 400+ systems.
Not allowed: invented stats, "AI", guarantees, response times, "best/leading", competitor names in ad copy (keywords on competitor names are fine), any number not listed here or in site-brief.md.
No em dashes anywhere.
